# Privacy Policy for Vision Aid

**Effective Date:** June 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to Vision Aid. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our web and mobile applications.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect the minimum amount of data necessary to provide our services:
*   **Account Information:** Name, email address, and profile picture (via Google Login).
*   **Usage Data:** Anonymized metrics on feature usage (e.g., color detection frequency) to improve our AI models.
*   **Camera Data:** Real-time video feed is processed locally or temporarily in memory for color/object detection. **WE DO NOT RECORD OR STORE YOUR VIDEO FEEDS.**

## 3. How We Use Your Information
*   To provide accessibility features (color detection, scene description).
*   To authenticate your identity.
*   To improve application performance and fix bugs.

## 4. Data Storage and Security
*   User preferences are stored securely in our database.
*   We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL) for all data transmission.
*   We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## 5. Your Rights
You have the right to:
*   Access the personal data we hold about you.
*   Request correction or deletion of your data.
*   Opt-out of optional analytics tracking.
